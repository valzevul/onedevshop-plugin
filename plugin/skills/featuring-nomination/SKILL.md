# App Store featuring nomination

Facts follow Apple's pages "Getting featured on the App Store" and "Nominate your app for featuring", read on 11 November 2026.

## The basics

- A Featuring Nomination tells Apple's editors what is about to be released. Apple names three kinds: new content, app enhancements and an app launch.
- The Account Holder, or a user with the Admin, App Manager or Marketing role, can submit one.
- Lead time: Apple's help page recommends at least three weeks. Its featuring page asks for at least two weeks and suggests up to three months for wider consideration. Plan for three weeks or more.
- Apple says there is no checklist. It lists what its team considers: user experience, UI design, innovation, uniqueness, accessibility, localization and the product page.
- Every app and game is eligible for App of the Day and Game of the Day, new or old.

## The steps

In App Store Connect: Apps, the app, Featuring, Nominations, the add button, Create Nomination. Name it, choose the kind, describe what is coming, pick the publish date or range, and add related apps, platforms, countries and regions, an In-App Event and links to supporting material. Submit it or save it as a draft. Several nominations at once go in as a CSV file, which submits them straight away with no drafts.

## Writing the description

Apple asks for the purpose, the priority and specific details of the update.

1. Open with a one-line outline of the app: what it is and who it is for.
2. Then what is new in this release and why it matters to the people who use it.
3. Then what stands out: a technique, an Apple framework used well, accessibility work, the languages it supports, a story behind it.
4. Keep claims checkable. Numbers need a source the user can show.
5. No superlatives the editors cannot verify, and no marketing filler.

Draft it, show the user the full text and let them submit it. Never submit a nomination yourself.

## If Apple picks the app

Apple may email the people with the Admin, App Manager and Marketing roles to ask for promotional artwork. Remind the user to make sure those addresses are read.

With the `docket` tools, `list_nominations` shows the nominations pulled from App Store Connect and `featuring_checks` checks a draft against the guidelines.
